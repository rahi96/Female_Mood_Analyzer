# Neumera — Features List

🆕 = new · 🔧 = upgrade existing

---

## Cycle & Fertility 🔧
- Fertile window prediction (shown as a range, not one exact day)
- "Avoiding pregnancy" mode (awareness only — not birth control)
- Hormone test upload and analysis

## Beauty & Radiance 🔧
- Skin health tracking (hydration, glow, texture)
- Skin scan result (compared to her own past scans)
- Skin vs sleep/cycle correlation

## Athlete 🆕
- Daily readiness score (0–100)
- HRV, training load, and recovery tracking
- Fatigue alerts
- Cycle-based training suggestions

## Pregnancy & Postpartum 🆕
- Pregnancy week tracker + baby development
- Doctor checkpoint timeline
- Warning-sign alerts (routes to care)
- Postpartum recovery tracking
- Mood check-in (postpartum depression screening)
- Pregnancy loss support

## Menopause 🆕
- Hot flash logging
- Symptom connection insights (hot flash → sleep → mood)
- Menopause stage tracker
- Mood support
- Intimate/urinary health tracking
- Doctor visit helper

## Lifelong Thriving 🆕
- Multi-year vitality trend
- Life-arc view (whole health history in one line)
- Mobility trends
- Cognitive wellness (habits, not testing)
- Preventative screening reminders

---

## AI Coach (Ask Neumera) 🔧
- Text + voice questions
- Structured answers (what I see → what it means → what to consider → journey insight → when to see a doctor)

## Other App Features
- Health dashboard + daily insight 🔧
- Trends (7-day / 30-day + correlations) 🔧
- Community "Fight Sisters" 🆕
- Share doctor report 🆕
